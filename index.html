Shared lists make matching possible

A buyer’s Preferred areas and Non-negotiables use exactly the same lists as a property’s Area and Features (solar, backup water, staff accommodation, pool, home office and so on). Matching a buyer to a listing is then like for like today, and can be automated later in Phase 7.

    What Happens Automatically

    Less Admin.
    Same Control.

    Phase 1 automations keep the structure tidy: records move to the right group, the right person is told, and deals keep the property up to date. Reminders and alerts come in Phase 2.

    Still manual in Phase 1, on purpose

    Setting a property to Under offer / Applying when a deal starts (a deal often exists before the property is linked) · changing a contact’s Search stage · typing the mandate expiry from the signed mandate · tenant vetting · copying the registration date to the property.

    What monday can’t do natively

    It can’t block a viewing for an unvetted tenant, so the Viewings board shows the vetting status instead. WhatsApp and Property24 enquiries are entered by hand for now. Automatic capture is assessed in Phase 2 and priced separately.

    Expected usage: roughly 150–400 automation actions a month for a three-person team, which fits comfortably inside the Pro plan allowance. We’ll confirm the current figure with monday.

    Access & POPIA

    Your Clients’ Data,
    Kept Close.

    Client and property records are personal information under POPIA. Phase 1 sets sensible defaults; your compliance adviser owns the policy.

    Closed workspace, no guests

    Only your three users. No guests and no public board links in Phases 1 to 6.

    Confirming on your Pro plan

    One admin, full-edit team

    Jessica as account admin and board owner (to confirm). The other two can edit everything, so a three-person team can cover for each other.

    Restricted columns

    Commission %, Commission (R) and Access / alarm notes visible to the admin only. Two-factor sign-in for all users.

    Confirming on your Pro plan

    POPIA habits built in

    A POPIA consent status on every enquiry and contact, and on every form. ID and FICA documents go in the FICA column only, never in updates or WhatsApp. Closed contacts are reviewed yearly.

    Open form
    Two things to know

    A form always creates a new record, so if the person is already a contact you merge the two. The questionnaire emails are sent through Gmail in the demo; in your account they’ll go through your own email provider (see question Q-11).

    Scope

    What Phase 1 Covers,
    and What It Leaves Out

    Each of these was left out on purpose so the workspace stays simple. Most already has a planned phase.

    Built in Phase 1

    • Six linked boards covering enquiries, contacts, properties, viewings, sales and rentals
    • Your stages as statuses, with records moving to the right group automatically
    • Buyer & tenant requirements captured as filterable columns, plus rental vetting and buyer finance
    • Mandate tracking with a days-left countdown and a 30-day expiry view
    • Commission calculated on every deal (rules to confirm)
    • A one-screen team dashboard and saved views for daily follow-ups
    • Training and written handover notes once it’s in your account

    Deliberately not in Phase 1

    • A separate tasks board. Next action + date on every record does the job.
    • Reminders for follow-ups, mandate expiries, lease ends and viewings P2
    • Document checklists and the full online forms rollout P3
    • Marketing checklist and the monthly owner report P4
    • Inspections, meters, keys and the 14-day snag list P5
    • KPI dashboards and agent scorecards P6
    • WhatsApp, Property24, Sims, Lightstone, FNB app, auto-matching, AI-assisted CMA P7
    • HubSpot migration or bulk loading of old records. Quoted separately if wanted.
